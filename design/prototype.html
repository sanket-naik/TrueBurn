Done does the work

One tap, from the lock screen, and the log updates itself.

Tick the water reminder and 500 ml lands on Today — watch the wave rise on the left. No second entry, no opening the app.

Tick the gym reminder and the progress bar moves and nothing else does. No calories are credited, ever — that would double-count against measured expenditure.

Skip is as prominent as Done. A reminder you can dismiss honestly is one you keep.

What this prototype decides

Information, not decoration

The adherence view is a timeline, not two bars

Counts tell you that you missed two reminders. A timeline tells you that you always miss the 15:00 one — which is the finding you can act on. Yesterday and today share an axis, so the pattern reads down the column.

Fewer entries

Each reminder is ticked individually

Completion is per reminder, not per day, so ticking three of six water reminders logs 750 ml — not all or nothing. It also means the timeline can show exactly which ones landed.

Three taps

Food opens on recents, not on search

People eat a small repertoire, so the list you already used is the fastest path. Quantity scales the calories live. Custom foods are saved as reusable items — §5.1 requires it, because re-guessing a portion each time biases the expenditure measurement downward.

Overrides spec §7

Your own reminders escape the 4/day cap

The cap exists for pings you did not ask for. A routine you built is not one. The sheet discloses the true cost before you commit, and says plainly when a schedule is dense enough that people usually mute it.
